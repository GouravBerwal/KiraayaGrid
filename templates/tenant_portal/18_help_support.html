Tenant Safety & Protection Tips

Follow these essential guidelines to ensure safe tenancy and seamless room handover.

1
Always Inspect in Person:
Never transfer money prior to physically visiting the property and meeting the building manager.
2
Check Government UIDAI Badges:
Ensure your listing displays the green "Verified Owner" UIDAI/ITD compliance seal.
3
Model Tenancy Act Contract:
Always obtain a digitally generated tenancy slip specifying deposit and refund terms.
4
Emergency Escalation:
If any landlord requests cash deposits without receipts, report immediately to our concierge.
