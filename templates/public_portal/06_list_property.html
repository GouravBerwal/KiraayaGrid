Frequently Asked Questions by Hosts

Is listing my property really 100% free? +

Yes. KiraayaGrid never charges landlords listing fees, subscription fees, or brokerage commissions. We believe property owners shouldn't have to surrender half a month's rent just to find quality residents.

How does KiraayaGrid verify prospective tenants? +

Every tenant on KiraayaGrid undergoes automated KYC with Government ID (Aadhaar verification) and corporate work email or college ID verification. You have complete visibility over their profile before you approve any walkthrough visit.

Can I set my own rent and house rules? +

Absolutely. You have 100% control over room pricing, gate timings, food preferences, guest policies, smoking/alcohol rules, and security deposit terms.

How does rent collection and payout work? +

Tenants pay their monthly rent through the KiraayaGrid tenant portal using UPI, Credit Card, or Net Banking. Funds are automatically transferred directly to your verified bank account by the 5th of each month, along with digital tax invoices.
