KiraayaGrid Community Guidelines

Official moderation standards & dispute resolution criteria

✓ 1. Genuine Interaction Requirement
Mandatory

Reviews must be authored by verified tenants, active enquirers, or verified visitors who scheduled a property visit through KiraayaGrid. Unverified third-party reviews are filtered out.

🚫 2. Zero Tolerance for Abusive Content
Immediate Removal

Personal harassment, hate speech, threats, vulgarity, discrimination based on gender/religion/caste, or defamatory attacks are strictly prohibited and immediately taken down.

🔒 3. Privacy & Personal Information Protection
Strict Redaction

Publishing personal phone numbers, UPI handles, bank details, or private family information of caretakers, owners, or other flatmates is grounds for immediate redaction.

⚖️ 4. Constructive Grievances are Protected
Protected Speech

Factual negative feedback regarding maintenance, security deposit delays, cleanliness, or power issues will NOT be removed simply because the property owner disputes it.
