KiraayaGrid Change Management Policy

To protect tenants against bait-and-switch pricing or sudden changes in living conditions, all property modifications are governed by the following strict rules:

1. Rent Adjustments (±10% Cap)
Any rent hike above 10% requires a 30-day notice to existing tenants and proof of major amenity upgrades.
2. Room & Bed Configuration
Adding rooms or altering sharing configuration requires an updated municipal floor layout certificate.
3. Address & Relocation Locks
Physical address changes are strictly locked to prevent transferring verified badges to unverified buildings.
4. 24-Hour Review SLA
All change requests must be approved, rejected, or put into review within 24 working hours.
